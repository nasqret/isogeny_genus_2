# Article Structure

1. Introduction and effectivity problem.
2. General method on the symmetric square of a genus-2 curve.
3. Construction of the complementary genus-one curve.
4. Divisor and interpolation algorithm for the complementary map.
5. Critical quartic polynomials and self-fiber products.
6. Explicit examples in degrees 3, 4, and 5.
7. Degree-2 families, `j`-invariants, and quadratic twists.

The source-of-truth article is `sources/paper.tex`; the archived PDF is
`sources/arxiv-2606.02429.pdf`.
